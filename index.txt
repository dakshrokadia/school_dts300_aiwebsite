Artificial intelligence, explained properly
No jargon, no hype, and nothing assumed.
Artificial intelligence turns up in the news every day, usually described either as the answer to everything or the end of the world. This site explains what it actually is, where it is already being used, and what people are arguing about, in language that does not assume you have seen any of it before.
